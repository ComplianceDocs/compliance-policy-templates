# Free Compliance Policy Templates

Open-source, complete, editable compliance policy templates — plain Markdown here, editable **Word (.docx)** versions in [`templates/`](templates/). No email gate, no signup.

Maintained by [ComplianceDocs](https://compliancedocshq.com), a maker of editable compliance documentation toolkits (ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 42001, AI governance, WISP).

Each template is a complete document — purpose with the exact regulatory citation, scope, policy statements, roles, enforcement and review — with `[bracketed placeholders]` for everything organization-specific.

## How to use

1. Download the `.docx` (or copy the Markdown) for the policy you need.
2. Replace every `[bracketed placeholder]` with how your organization actually operates.
3. Delete anything that does not apply; a policy that describes practices you don't follow is worse than no policy in an audit.
4. Route it through your approval process and record version, owner, and review date in the document-control block.

**The honest caveat:** no template, free or paid, makes an organization certified or compliant. ISO 27001 certification comes only from an accredited body's audit; SOC 2 is a licensed CPA firm's attestation; HIPAA compliance is operating the safeguards. Templates remove the drafting — you still run the program.
